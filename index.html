The moves

  • Venue changed on August 21. Not Mateo Warehouse. The Compound, 2909 Supply Avenue, Commerce, off the 5 and the 710. Put that in the rideshare, not "DNBNL".
  • Three VIP+ wristbands on Gordon's DICE. One phone holds all three at the door, so nobody goes in first. Stage access means the rail is not the only place to stand.
  • It starts in daylight. September in Commerce is hot at four. Water before the first drop, shade between sets, the outdoor stage has the sun on it until seven.
  • Sunday is not a rest day. Gordon has a BlizzCon pass for Sunday in Anaheim, thirty minutes south. Last set, home, sleep, murlocs.
  • Thirteen days later: Portola. David hosts in San Francisco, September 25 to 27. This is the warm-up.

Why this one

DNBNL started as Brownies & Lemonade's drum and bass night in 2021 and this is the first time they have tried to shut down a block for it. Gordon's own record with this music runs through Sub Focus three times in a year, Dimension at HARD Summer, and Countdown on the last night of 2025. David's first rave was a gift from Gordon in 2018, and he was in the house for Sub Focus last October. Trent's first EDC was this May with this crew. The three of you at one drum and bass stage is new. That is the whole point of Saturday.
